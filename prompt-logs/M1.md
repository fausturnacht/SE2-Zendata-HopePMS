## **Entry 7**  
**Date:** May 4, 2026   
**Task:** Improving visual responsiveness of the app, removing jittering, preserving tab states

### **1st prompt given to AI:**  
“There seems to be an issue with loading the pages and switching to other browser tabs.

* Loading the website, through a refresh, seemingly always returns to dashboard no matter what page you are on.  
* There is a brief second where the app visually flickers for a few times before returning to normal.  
* If you navigate anywhere while this is happening, the app goes completely white.  
* The app also goes white and shows nothing if you navigate to another browser tab and go back.”

**What the AI produced:**  
The AI performed a deep-dive analysis of the authentication and permission lifecycle. It identified a race condition where ProtectedRoute was rendering pages before UserRightsContext had finished fetching permissions, causing premature redirects to the dashboard. It also found that tab-switching triggered redundant loading states in the context providers.

The AI produced a comprehensive fix that:

* Synchronized ProtectedRoute to wait for both Auth and User Rights initialization.  
* Optimized UserRightsProvider to track permissions by userid, preventing state "flips" and flickers on focus/tab-switch.  
* Stabilized AuthProvider initialization to ensure a consistent session state across refreshes.

**What I changed / improved:**  
None

**What I learned / decided:**  
AI-coded web-apps seem to struggle with tab switching bugs, at least in the early iterations.
